FINAL CHOICE MAID AGENCY — PRIVATE REVIEW COPY
==============================================

This is the complete Final Choice website with the real maid biodata, for management review only.
It is NOT published anywhere. Every maid card says "LOCAL REVIEW - NOT PUBLISHED".

CONFIDENTIAL: it contains real maids' names, photos, ages and religion. Please do not copy it,
forward it, post it online or show it to anyone outside the company. Delete it when the review is finished.

HOW TO OPEN (Windows — nothing to install, no sign-in, no internet needed)
  1. Right-click the zip and choose "Extract All" (or use 7-Zip if the zip is password-protected),
     so that you have a normal folder.
  2. Open the folder and double-click  START-REVIEW.bat
  3. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" then "Run anyway".
     If a black window opens and your browser opens the website, it is working.
  4. Keep the black window open while browsing. Close it to stop the website.

  English site:           http://localhost:4330/en/
  Bahasa Melayu site:     http://localhost:4330/ms/
  (If another program already uses that number, the black window shows the number it chose instead.)

HOW TO OPEN (Mac, or if the .bat file is blocked)
  Open Terminal in the "site" folder and run:   python3 -m http.server 4330
  Then open http://localhost:4330/en/ in your browser.

WHAT TO LOOK AT
  - Find a Maid: all 52 candidates (41 Indonesian, 11 Filipino) with search, filters and pages.
    Indonesian / Filipino pages show each nationality. Click a card for the full profile.
  - Packages: three categories (Indonesian, Indonesian Christian, Filipino).
  - Switch language with the EN / BM button at the top.
  - Availability shows "Available", "Not yet confirmed" notes or "Availability to be confirmed": statuses are not
    verified yet. WhatsApp buttons open a real chat with the company number — please do not press them
    for test enquiries.
